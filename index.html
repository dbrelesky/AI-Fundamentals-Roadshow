If You Remember One Thing
AI isn’t trying to replace you…but it will shift how you work.
It changes what a small team can get done.
Creates a stronger demand for high leverage individuals.

You stay the editor, the taste, the judgment. AI takes the grunt work so you spend more time on the ideas fans actually feel.

Demystify

Think of AI as a fast, tireless junior collaborator.

  • Reads anything in seconds: a 40-page deck, a research packet, a transcript.
  • Writes a first draft of almost anything you ask for.
  • Makes things now: images, simple sites, edits, options at volume.
  • Talks like a person. No code, no jargon. You just ask.

IT IS NOT

  • a search engine
  • magic, or sentient
  • always right

You manage it like a sharp intern: clear direction in, you edit what comes out.

Set Expectations

Where it shines, and where to keep your hands on the wheel

GREAT AT

  • First drafts and starting points
  • Summarizing long, messy material
  • Options at volume (20 headlines, 10 concepts)
  • Rewrites, tone shifts, tightening copy
  • Research head-starts and brainstorming
  • Turning a rough idea into something you can see

KEEP YOUR HANDS ON THE WHEEL

  • It makes things up, confidently. Verify facts, names, stats.
  • It has no taste. Your eye is the product.
  • Don’t paste anything confidential into random tools.
  • First answer is rarely the best, push it, redirect it.
  • It’s a starting line, not a finish line.

My Honest Bias

Why I lean on Claude

  • It finishes what it starts. The most autonomy and accountability to see a task through, and the most interactive: it asks you questions as it works instead of guessing.
  • Three tools, one workbench. Claude chat to think and draft, Cowork to act on your files and tools, Claude Code to build. Same brain, three surfaces.
  • It bends to how you work. The most adaptable across creative and developer workflows: web, desktop app, mobile, and command-line.
  • The most capable model right now: Opus 4.7 Top tier at both thinking and doing. (OpenAI’s Codex is not far behind.)
The Claude Workbench

Three tools, same brain

Claude chat

WEB · DESKTOP · MOBILE APP
The conversational assistant. You type, it answers.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Draft, rewrite, summarize, brainstorm
  • Answer questions, analyze what you paste
  • Make images and quick one-offs

WHO IT’S FOR

Everyone. The zero-setup front door to AI.

Cowork

DESKTOP APP
Chat that does the work, on your real files and tools.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Works across your files, Slack, email, calendar, Drive
  • Builds decks, docs and sheets; automates busywork
  • Runs on a schedule and connects your apps

WHO IT’S FOR

Non-developers: marketing, design, writers, strategy. (It’s what I built this deck in.)

Claude Code

COMMAND LINE / TERMINAL
An agent that builds software for you.

WHAT IT DOES

  • Builds apps, sites, scripts and automations
  • Most configurable: plan-first or full autonomy
  • Turns “I wish we had a tool” into a real tool

WHO IT’S FOR

Developers and the technically curious who want to build.

Industry Outlook

What AI does to the categories we compete in

Two shifts: making content gets near-free, and the fan experience gets personalized. The moat moves from volume to rights, trust, and taste.

2026 — Happening Now ~2028 — Where It Heads
Live sports & production Near-real-time highlights and clips; auto-captions and fast localization. Personalized feeds and camera angles; instant clips in every format, per fan.
Streaming & on-demand Sharper recommendations; AI dubbing and search across the catalog. The home screen assembles itself around each viewer.
Content, recaps & social Recaps, social cutdowns and research drafted at volume; humans edit. Content gets near-free; the edge is a distinct POV and trust, not volume.
Fantasy, betting & games Conversational stats and start/sit help; smarter personalized advice. An always-on companion that knows your teams, bets and history.
Our move: use AI to deepen fan trust and personalization, not to flood feeds with cheap content.
The Demand Side

How AI changes what fans expect

Behaviors we’re already seeing, and where they head.

What We’re Seeing Now · 2026

  • Fans ask AI instead of searching, fewer clicks to publishers
  • Summary-first: “just tell me who won and why”
  • Talking to assistants conversationally, second-screen all game
  • Rising “is this even real?” doubt as AI content floods feeds

What May Come · ~2028

  • A personal AI companion that knows your teams, fantasy and bets
  • Content comes to you, auto-shaped to your format and length
  • Ask, don’t browse: conversation becomes how fans discover
  • Trust premium: verified sources and real talent win attention
What it means: fans will expect answers, personalization, and proof. Our edge is real access, real talent, and trust no generic bot can fake.
