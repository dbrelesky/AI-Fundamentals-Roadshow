Live Introduction

AI isn’t trying to replace us.... but it will shift how we work.

It changes what a small team can get done. Creates a stronger demand for high-leverage individuals. You stay the editor, the taste, the judgment. AI takes the grunt work so you spend more time on the ideas fans actually feel.

Demystify

Think of AI as a crew of tireless junior collaborators who are reeeeeeeaallly smart.

What it is
  • Reads anything in seconds: a 40-page deck, a research packet, a 5-yr LRP, a complete codebase.
  • Writes a first draft of almost anything you ask for.
  • Makes things now: images, simple sites, edits, options at volume.
  • Talks like a person. No code, no jargon. You just ask.
It is not
  • a search engine
  • magic, or sentient
  • always right

💡 You manage it like a sharp intern: clear direction in, you edit what comes out.

Set Expectations

Where it shines, and where to keep your hands on the wheel.

👍 Great at

  • First drafts of memos, board slides, and earnings talking points
  • Summarizing 10-Ks, earnings calls, and long rights or contract packets
  • Options at volume (20 variance narratives, 10 scenario framings)
  • Turning a rough ask into a model, chart, or one-pager you can stress-test

🪂 Keep your hands on the wheel

  • Hallucinates numbers and citations. Verify every figure, source, and formula.
  • No judgment on materiality or what leadership needs to hear. Your call on what ships.
  • Don’t paste earnings, forecasts, M&A, or personnel into unapproved tools.
  • Starting point for the model or memo, not the sign-off.

Industry Outlook

What AI does to the categories we compete in.

Making content gets near-free; the fan experience gets personalized. The moat moves from volume to rights, trust, and taste.

2026 · Happening Now ~2028 · Where It Heads
Live sports & production Near-real-time highlights and clips; auto-captions and fast localization. Personalized feeds and camera angles; instant clips in every format, per fan.
Streaming & on-demand Sharper recommendations; AI dubbing and search across the catalog. The home screen assembles itself around each viewer.
Content, recaps & social Recaps, social cutdowns and research drafted at volume; humans edit. Content gets near-free; the edge is a distinct POV and trust, not volume.
Fantasy, betting & games Conversational stats and start/sit help; smarter personalized advice. An always-on companion that knows your teams, bets and history.

Our move: use AI to deepen fan trust and personalization, not to flood feeds with cheap content.

The Demand Side

How AI changes what fans expect.

Behaviors we’re already seeing, and where they head.

What we’re seeing now · 2026

  • Fans ask AI instead of searching, fewer clicks to publishers
  • Summary-first: “just tell me who won and why”
  • Talking to assistants conversationally, second-screen all game
  • Rising “is this even real?” doubt as AI content floods feeds

What may come · ~2028

  • A personal AI companion that knows your teams, fantasy and bets
  • Content comes to you, auto-shaped to your format and length
  • Ask, don’t browse: conversation becomes how fans discover
  • Trust premium: verified sources and real talent win attention

What it means: fans will expect answers, personalization, and proof. Our edge is real access, real talent, and trust no generic bot can fake.
